Four machines. Four kinds of legacy.

Filter by destination, then open each story. “Resting” hardware can still change science through the data it sent home.

MOON · APOLLO 11

Passive Seismic Experiment

Silent hardware

The first seismometer placed on the Moon listened for moonquakes and impacts, giving scientists an early look inside the Moon.

  • Powered by two solar-cell panels
  • Returned data for about three weeks
  • Later Apollo stations transmitted until 1977
MARS · CURIOSITY

A Laboratory on Wheels

Active mission

Curiosity drills and studies rock in Gale Crater to ask whether ancient Mars had conditions suitable for microbial life.

  • 17 cameras and 10 science instruments
  • Landed in August 2012
  • Includes SAM, CheMin, ChemCam and RAD
INTERSTELLAR SPACE · VOYAGER

Messages Beyond the Sun

Extended mission

The Voyager twins became the only spacecraft to operate beyond the heliosphere. Each carries a 12-inch gold-plated copper record.

  • Launched in 1977
  • Voyager 1 crossed into interstellar space in 2012
  • Golden Record includes sounds, images and 55 greetings
KUIPER BELT · NEW HORIZONS

The Pluto Pathfinder

Active mission

New Horizons revealed Pluto up close, then flew past Arrokoth, the most distant object ever explored at close range.

  • Pluto flyby: 14 July 2015
  • Arrokoth flyby: 1 January 2019
  • Powered by a radioisotope generator
